01 — Foundation

Why Social Media Matters

Attract Sponsors

Sponsors search online before committing. A strong presence shows professionalism and community impact, which helps to secure funding for build season.

Build Community

Engage with other FRC teams, local schools, and STEM advocates. Networking leads to mentorships and resource sharing across the ecosystem.

Recruit Members

Students and mentors discover teams through social media. Showcasing our culture attracts passionate engineers, programmers, and future team members.

02b — New Platform

Why LinkedIn?

01
Professional Credibility

Pitching on LinkedIn presents a highly serious, business-minded image compared to casual platforms.

02
Demonstrating ROI

Sharing milestones, CAD designs, and outreach proves to sponsors that their funding directly builds the future STEM workforce.

03
Sponsor Recognition

Tagging sponsors gives them valuable, public, and professional acknowledgment within the business community.

04
Alumni Network

Easily track and maintain connections with graduated students as they enter college and industry.

05
Targeted Mentor Recruitment

Post specific "Mentor Wanted" listings to recruit specialized professionals like machinists or software developers.

06
Award & Judge Appeal

A public archive of sustainability and outreach that proves to judges we prioritize real-world career readiness.
